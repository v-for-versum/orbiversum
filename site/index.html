Orbiversum

A drone synthesizer platform for ESP32-S3 boards with a round display. Audio and UI are no_std Rust: the same voices run on the device, in tests and in a command-line WAV renderer.

Flash your boardSource on GitHub

What it does

An applet menu with two drones, ported from SuperCollider drones of the Dronecaster project.

Starlids

Twelve slowly drifting lanes of detuned saws with sweeping lowpass, a wandering delay and random panning, plus a sub pulse, through a Moog ladder filter.

Eno

A slow generative “music for airports” piece: an 8-voice pad, sub sines, a Moog filter, piano-string and PolyPerc layers, a Klank resonator and reverb.

Controls

Turn the knobpitch up/down by a semitone (20–2000 Hz)
Tap top halflevel up
Tap bottom halflevel down
Hold a finger 600 msopen the applet menu

Hardware

Guition JC3636K718C: ESP32-S3R8, 8 MB PSRAM, 16 MB flash, 360×360 round display, touch, I2S DAC (PCM5100A). Connect the DAC line output to an amplifier or headphones. The USB port is used for flashing and the serial log.
